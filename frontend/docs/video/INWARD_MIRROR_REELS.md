# Inward Mirror: reel scripts (7 x about 30 seconds)

Every spoken or on-screen line is quoted word for word from the booklet's **free chapters** (the opening and chapters 1 to 3), so a viewer who clicks through can read the very sentence they saw before any sign-up. A script checked every line against the free text when this file was made.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 28 to 32 seconds. Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels.

**Look:** dark and unhurried, in the brand colours (ink #0f0e0c, gold #c4a96b, parchment #e8e0d4). Slow camera, candle and window light, no fast cuts. Cover art of the booklet is the starting image where it helps.

**Sound:** a low, steady tanpura-like drone, no lyrics, no beat. Optional calm male voice reading the lines at a measured pace. Quote lines appear on screen in a serif face (Playfair Display / Crimson Pro), gold on dark.

**Tone:** sober and non-guru. No promises of peace or transformation; the work is the question.

## Timing for every reel

| Seconds | What |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 | HOOK: the first line appears and is spoken. It has to work with sound off. |
| 3 to 20 | BEATS: one or two lines, each held for 6 to 8 seconds. |
| 20 to 27 | PAYOFF: the closing line, held longest. |
| 27 to 30 | END CARD: series, booklet number, "Read the first chapters free", link in bio. |
